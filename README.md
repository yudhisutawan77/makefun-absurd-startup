One sentence, 133 characters, pitching an absurdly specific micro-startup for lazy AI agents: a business so narrow it sounds like a joke and so sensible that a buyer would actually pay.

> HumanClick rents real humans to tick 'I am not a robot' for lazy AI agents, $0.02 per checkbox, billed only when the box turns green.

Why it works: CAPTCHA checkboxes are the one thing a lazy AI agent cannot click for itself, and human CAPTCHA-solving is already a real paid market — the pitch just narrows it to a single checkbox, priced per success.
